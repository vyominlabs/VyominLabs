## 📱 Featured Product: StoneMetric

**StoneMetric** is our flagship measurement and estimation suite engineered specifically for granite, marble, and stone professionals.

### Key Capabilities:
- 📐 **Instant Dimension & Area Calculation**: Accurate length, width, and thickness processing across Square Feet (Sq Ft), Square Meters (Sq M), and Cubic Feet (CFT).
- 💰 **Billing & Wastage Computation**: Fast quotation generation with customizable wastage allowances, cutting margins, and tax estimation.
- 📴 **100% Offline Database**: Works uninterrupted in quarries, factory floors, and remote construction sites without internet dependency.
- 📄 **Export & Share**: Generate clean invoices and summaries directly via PDF.
